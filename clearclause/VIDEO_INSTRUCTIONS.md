# ClearClause - Submission Video Guide

This guide breaks down exactly how to record your 4-minute submission video for the ClearClause project to score maximum points according to the judging criteria.

## Prerequisites Before Recording
- Ensure the app is running smoothly locally or deployed.
- Have a sample legal document ready (e.g., a sample NDA, Terms of Service, or Employment Contract) with clear clauses.
- Plan a script or on-screen captions so you don't stall during the video.
- **Keep it under 4 minutes!** Use a timer while practicing.

## Video Structure & Walkthrough Steps

### 1. Introduction & Context (0:00 - 0:20)
- Briefly state the problem: "Legal contracts are hard to understand for regular people."
- Introduce ClearClause and the core solution.

### 2. Live Testing: Document Upload (0:20 - 0:50)
- **Action**: Show the homepage and upload your sample legal document live.
- **Criteria checked**: "Enter data live", "Show success and edge cases".
- **Tip**: Pause briefly so the judges can see the file name and type being uploaded.
- **Optional Edge Case**: If you have time, quickly try to upload an unsupported file (e.g., an image instead of a PDF/text) to show the error handling.

### 3. GenAI in Action: Scan Risks (0:50 - 1:40)
- **Action**: Click the "Scan Risks" feature.
- **Criteria checked**: "Point out exactly where and how AI powers your solution", "Prove it's dynamic".
- **Tip**: Say or show text explaining: "Here, Google Gemini is analyzing the text to identify potential liabilities." Show the dynamic output generated by the AI based on the *specific* document you uploaded.

### 4. GenAI in Action: Q&A (Ask AI) (1:40 - 2:30)
- **Action**: Use the Ask AI feature. Type a question live, such as "What happens if I break confidentiality?"
- **Criteria checked**: "Show the prompt going in and the response coming out." 
- **Tip**: Type the input *live* and clearly visible. Wait for the AI to answer. Show that the answer isn't hardcoded but extracted and interpreted from the document by the Generative AI.

### 5. GenAI in Action: Generate Brief & Export (2:30 - 3:30)
- **Action**: Click "Generate Brief" and let the AI summarize the contract.
- **Action**: Click Export to show that the AI-generated results can be taken offline.
- **Tip**: This shows a complete workflow from upload to final usable output.

### 6. Closing (3:30 - 4:00)
- Summarize the value and impact of the app.
- Ensure you stop recording before the 4-minute mark.

## Final Review Checklist
- [ ] Is the entire video under 4 minutes?
- [ ] Did I type queries and upload documents *live* on screen?
- [ ] Did I clearly explain/show when Gemini (GenAI) was being used?
- [ ] Are the input values clearly readable by the judges?
- [ ] Is the video link set to "Public" (Google Drive) or "Unlisted/Public" (YouTube)?
- [ ] Did I test the final link in an Incognito window?
